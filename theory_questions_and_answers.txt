Answer:
When a user listens to indie-pop tracks late in the evening, Spotify tracks implicit feedback metrics such as song completion rates, skips, and repeat listens. Using matrix factorization and audio feature clustering (analyzing tempo, energy, and acousticness), the recommendation engine identifies similar tracks enjoyed by users with matching taste profiles. It then dynamically populates the user's 'Discover Weekly' playlist with under-the-radar songs that fit their late-night listening mood. This data-driven personalization increases user engagement and session duration without manual playlist curation.

Answer:
- Chosen Industry: Sports
- Application: Computer Vision & Spatial Analytics (Optical Tracking)
- Description: Professional sports leagues (such as the NBA, Premier League, and IPL) use high-speed optical camera arrays (e.g., Hawk-Eye, Second Spectrum) and computer vision algorithms to track 2D/3D coordinates of players and the ball 25+ times per second. [Task 5]
Question: Use ChatGPT to suggest 3 new features that could be engineered from a dataset of online food orders (like Zomato orders: order time, restaurant, cuisine, delivery time, rating). List the features and explain how each could help a data science model.

Answer:
1. Is_Peak_Meal_Hour (Binary Flag: 1 if order time is between 12:00-14:30 or 19:00-22:00, else 0):
   - Model Benefit: Captures peak kitchen rush hours and driver surcharges, allowing ETA prediction models to automatically add buffer times during peak meal hours.
2. Historical_Prep_Speed_Ratio (Ratio of a restaurant's average preparation time compared to the overall city average prep time across all restaurants):
   - Model Benefit: Helps the model differentiate between fast-food outlets (fast prep) and fine-dining restaurants (longer prep) regardless of cuisine type.
3. Rating_vs_Cuisine_Avg (Difference between an individual restaurant's rating and the average rating of all restaurants serving the same cuisine):
   - Model Benefit: Normalizes rating bias across different food types (e.g., fast food vs. gourmet desserts), isolating genuine restaurant quality for recommendation models.
